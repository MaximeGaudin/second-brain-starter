# What is next

Gather first:

1. my open issues and their status
2. my calendar for the next 48 hours
3. anything in notes/ flagged as blocked
4. anything in notes/expectations.md I am waiting on from someone else

Rank by, in this order:

- someone else is blocked on me
- it expires or breaks if I wait
- it moves a quarterly objective
- everything else

Give me eight items. One line each, with the reason.

Anything that has slipped three days in a row, say so out loud instead of quietly ranking it lower again.

If my calendar leaves no room for the top item today, say that too. A list I cannot execute is not a plan.
