# How I write

This file is the only place my voice is defined. Every skill that writes something for me points here.

Rewrite all of it. The lines below are mine, not yours — they are here to show the level of detail that actually changes the output.

Short sentences. One idea each.

- Decision first, reasoning after. Never the reverse.
- No "I hope this finds you well". No "circling back". No "just wanted to check in".
- When the answer is no, it is in the first line, with the reason next to it.
- Numbers or nothing. If I do not have it, I say so.
- Sign off with my first name. Nothing else.
